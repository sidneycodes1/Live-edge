## Part 1 — Research summary (what this product actually is, from the code)

- **What it does.** LiveEdge is a watch-party + prediction app: it aggregates LIVE
  public streams (YouTube Data API search + iframe embeds, Twitch and Kick APIs, a
  Livepeer-hosted "floor" stream) and lets users place **simulated-money** bets on
  what happens in/near those live events. Markets are generated by an AI engine
  (Gemini) from live context and resolved against real-world data where available
  (`server/src/routes/live.js`, `markets.js`, `rooms.js`, `stream.js`,
  `docs/HOW_PANTA_IS_USED.md`, migration `008_gemini_market_engine.sql`).
- **Money.** NO real money exists anywhere in the product. Balances are integers in
  a Postgres/PGlite `balances` table ("Sim USD"); every account is created with a
  $100 welcome **mint** and free top-ups come from a play-money **faucet**
  (`server/src/routes/faucet.js`, `004_mint_events.sql`). There is **no payment
  processor, no deposits, no withdrawals, no purchase of sim funds** (verified:
  zero payment-rail env keys or SDKs in `server/src/config/env.js` / package deps).
  Fee/creator-share columns exist (`005_fee_events.sql`, `SIM_FEE_BPS`) but operate
  purely on simulated units.
- **Accounts & data collected.** Accounts are created ONLY by a Privy login
  (email OTP / Google — owner decision 2026-10-05); Privy provisions an embedded
  **Solana wallet** whose public address becomes `users.wallet` and the account's
  signing key (`server/src/routes/privy.js`, `011_user_profile.sql`, spec D1/Amend-3).
  We store: wallet address (bs58 public key — not a secret), display name, optional
  email (legacy routes), interests, privy DID, pins, orders/positions, chat
  messages, notifications, mint/fee journals. Sessions use a 1-hour JWT held in
  browser sessionStorage (`web/src/lib/api.js`). No third-party analytics package
  was found in `web/package.json` (verify before launch).
- **Custody.** The embedded wallet's key is held via Privy's infrastructure
  (custodial-style), not a local seed phrase the user manages. Our server only ever
  stores/uses the PUBLIC address for signature verification
  (`server/src/lib/signature.js`). The wallet currently signs LiveEdge messages
  only; it holds no transferable assets in this product.
- **UGC & AI content.** Users can send chat messages in rooms, create rooms, and
  (capability present) go live as streamers (`chat.js`, `rooms.js`, `streamer.js`).
  Market questions and an "AI spectator" are AI-generated and labeled as AI in the
  UI (honesty rules; `009_ai_spectator.sql`).
- **Third-party services actually integrated:** Privy (auth + embedded wallets),
  YouTube (Data API + player embeds), Twitch (Helix/API), Kick API, API-Football
  (match data), Livepeer Studio (floor stream), Google Gemini (AI generation).
  Their own terms apply to their parts of the experience.
- **Risk-relevant finding (load-bearing):** YouTube/Twitch platform terms restrict
  using their services to facilitate gambling/betting on their content. LiveEdge is
  play-money-only (no consideration, no prizes of value), which is the standard
  "social prediction" posture — but the overlaying of bets on embedded third-party
  video is exactly the area their policies scrutinize. Flagged in Part 4; this is a
  reason the product must never imply real wagering and must keep sim-money language
  perfect everywhere near embeds.
- **Stage.** Hackathon/MVP; deployments are demo-grade; settlement mode is
  simulated ("Real Panta data · simulated settlement" lineage, HOW_PANTA_IS_USED.md).

## Part 2 — Draft Terms & Conditions (human-readable, product-specific)

**Last updated: October 2026 · Version: 2026-10-draft-1**

### 1. What LiveEdge is — and what it firmly is not
LiveEdge is a place to watch live streams and sports with friends and bet **fake
money** on what happens next. You get $100 in **play money** ("Sim USD") when you
join, and a faucet hands out more whenever you run out. That is the whole economy.
Sim USD is a number in our database: **it has no cash value, it cannot be bought,
sold, withdrawn, transferred, or exchanged for anything real, and nothing you win
is ever redeemable.** LiveEdge is not a gambling site, not a sportsbook, not an
exchange, and not an investment product. If any screen ever makes it feel like
real money, that's a bug in our copy — tell us.

### 2. Who can use it
You must be **at least 18 years old**. We ask you to confirm this honestly; we do
not verify your age with documents. By using LiveEdge you also confirm that using
an app like this is legal where you live. If the laws where you are put live
prediction games (even free ones) in a grey area, please don't use LiveEdge — the
internet is big and we genuinely mean it when we say nothing here is worth anything.

### 3. Accounts, logins, and the little wallet we all pretend you don't have to think about
Accounts are created when you sign in through **Privy** with your email (one-time
code) or Google. Privy automatically creates an **embedded Solana wallet** for you;
LiveEdge uses its public address as your account identity and as the key that
"signs" your bets so the record is tamper-proof. You never need to write down a
seed phrase for LiveEdge to work, and the wallet in this app holds nothing of value.
Your Privy login and wallet recovery are governed by **Privy's terms** — keep your
email and Google account secure; you're responsible for activity on your login.
(Older email-password and guest accounts exist in our history; guest access has
been removed, and legacy password accounts can't sign in through the app anymore.)

### 4. Predictions, markets, and how things resolve
Other users, and an **AI engine that is labeled as AI**, write the market questions
you bet on. Live data (scores, streams, news feeds) comes from third parties and
**can be late, wrong, or missing**. When a market is ambiguous, broken, cancelled,
or built on a feed that glitched, we resolve or void it using the rules shown on
that market — final resolution calls are ours, made in good faith, and we show the
resolution basis on the market. Simulated winnings and losses mean exactly that:
numbers moving in your play-money balance. There is no house, no rake you pay in
real value, and no payouts in real value. Ever.

### 5. Your content and how you must behave
Chat messages, room names, display names, and any streams you broadcast stay
**yours** — you grant us a licence to display them inside LiveEdge. Don't use the
app to harass, hate, spam, share illegal content, scrape our data, bot the
faucet, try to fake results, or do anything that breaks the platform rules of the
video services we embed. We moderate and we remove, and we can act on accounts
without a right of appeal in hard cases — protecting real users beats a fake-money
leaderboard position, always.

### 6. Third parties, briefly named
The video you watch comes from **YouTube, Twitch, Kick, or Livepeer**; match data
from **API-Football**; log-in and embedded wallets from **Privy**; market
generation from **Google Gemini**. Each has its own terms, and they apply to their
slices of your experience. Streams can disappear at any moment — embeds are up to
the platform, not us. Interruptions are not a refund of anything, because there's
nothing to refund.

### 7. Privacy snapshot (see also the separate Privacy notice — TBD)
We store: your wallet public address, display name, email (via Privy/legacy), your
interest selections, pins, bets, chat messages, and notification history. Your
session token lives only in your browser tab. We found no third-party analytics
running in the app at draft time (re-verify at launch). We don't sell your data.
Retention/erasure self-service isn't built yet — email us to delete your account
and data, and we'll do it by hand until the button exists.

### 8. Intellectual property
The LiveEdge name, design, and code are ours. Third-party footage/logos belong to
their owners — we embed, we don't own. Your content is yours (see §5). AI-generated
market text is labeled as AI-generated.

### 9. Disclaimers
LiveEdge is provided "as is", currently as a hackathon-stage product. No guarantee

### 10. Limitation of liability

## Part 4 — ⚠ REQUIRES HUMAN / LEGAL REVIEW BEFORE USE (the most important section)

A draft written by engineering cannot responsibly decide these. Each one needs the
owner + qualified counsel (or a product decision) BEFORE this ToS ships publicly:

1. **Governing law & dispute venue (§12)** — deliberately left blank. Jurisdiction
   determines enforceability of almost everything else here.
2. **Wagering-adjacent regulatory analysis.** Even free-to-play prediction games
   with fake currency are regulated differently by US states/countries (some treat
   "social casino"/sweepstakes-style mechanics as gambling-adjacent; CFTC has
   publicly opined on prediction markets even when not cash-settled in some
   structures). Confirm LiveEdge's posture ("entertainment, no consideration, no
   prizes") holds in target launch jurisdictions.
3. **Third-party platform ToS compatibility — HIGH RISK ITEM.** YouTube's and
   Twitch's ToS restrict facilitating wagering on their services. The product must
   (a) never use their trademarks in ways implying sponsorship, (b) keep the sim
   boundary airtight in all copy near embeds, and (c) ideally get a quick counsel
   read before scaling traffic on this specific combination (live 3rd-party video
   + bet overlay). This is the single most likely cause of a takedown/ban, and a
   generic ToS would have missed it entirely.
4. **Age verification adequacy.** We *ask* (checkbox); we do not *verify*. Counsel
   must confirm that's acceptable for the chosen jurisdiction/store listing
   (app stores rate "simulated gambling" content; 17+/18+ ratings likely needed).
5. **Privy custody & wallet recovery.** Embedded wallet keys are custodial with
   Privy. If wallets ever gain real functions (assets, exports), custody language,
   recovery obligations, and possibly licensing questions change entirely. Revisit
   before ever touching anything real-valued. **Recommendation: never.**
6. **Privacy compliance depth.** No formal Privacy Notice page exists yet (Part 2 §7
   is a snapshot). GDPR/CCPA-grade disclosures (DPO, rights requests, retention
   schedule, cross-border transfer basis, cookie policy since we use
   localStorage/sessionStorage) need a dedicated privacy document, not a ToS clause.
7. **Data retention & deletion:** manual-by-email today (§7). A retention schedule
   and a delete-account endpoint must be committed to (with dates) before real
   users' emails sit in Postgres at scale.
8. **"Analytics absent" claim** — asserted from a package.json read at draft time;
   re-verify in the production build (no CDN scripts, no fonts with tracking, etc.).
9. **Contact/Entity placeholders (§14)** — must be a real responding address before
   publishing; several regimes require it.
10. **Fees columns / PANTA naming.** Internal naming (faucet, fee_events,
    "settlement") must never surface in user-facing copy in ways that imply real
    financial services. The AI/UI copy review should grep for: payout, withdraw,
    deposit, cash, odds, wager, real money.
