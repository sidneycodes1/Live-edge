# Sprout design system

LiveEdge uses one soft green market-garden direction: Night Garden. Tokens live in `web/src/index.css` and are exposed to Tailwind from `web/tailwind.config.js`.

- Display: Fredoka 600 for brand and headings only; Nunito for readable UI and market questions.
- Type scale: 12, 14, 16, 18, 22, 28, 36px. Inputs are always at least 16px.
- Shape: 8px controls, 20px cards, 28px sheets. Pills are for chips and primary actions only.
- Voice: warm, direct, factual. Say play money and price provenance plainly.
- Do: use `surface`, `ink`, `brand`, `yes`, `no`, and semantic token utilities.
- Do not: use fake activity, white selected rows, neutral-black surfaces, or color alone to convey YES/NO.
